FAQ

Where is my progress saved?
Only on your device. Deleting the game deletes your progress.

I'm stuck on a level.
Every level has at least one solution; the computer solves each level in advance to make sure. Try undo, a hint or a power-up.

How do I change the language?
Settings → Language.

Do I need to pay real money?
No. There are no real-money purchases in the game.
